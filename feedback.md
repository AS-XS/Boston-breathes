Project goal is clear and timeline seems manageable. One thing to think about is whether BlueBike activity is representative enough of seasonal population change? Specifically, would BlueBike activity really reflect the variation in Greater Boston's population change and city activity due to university students? How will university students be differentiated from local residents that use BlueBikes? What about number of people riding the T, traffic levels, or number of people using rideshare apps like Uber in different seasons/during university breaks? Try to come up with some other metrics to analyze the variance of city activity and population. As a result of that, you will need to find other sources of data to supplement those other metrics you come up with.
